## SceneKyaHai
- Conversation flow is a focused client state machine in src/routes/index.tsx with draft answers retained across steps; AI handles short reactions, explicit refinement patches and venue picks to keep navigation deterministic.
- Places/geocoding go through the Google Maps gateway in src/lib/places.server.ts; locations rounded to ~1 km — privacy requirement.
- A curation row is inserted only after a successful Plan A/B/C result; free status = zero rows for the device_id — never charge/count failures.
- Shared validated brief and pure eligibility rules live in src/lib/scene.ts, with tests beside them, so distance and country constraints are enforced beyond presentation.
- Opening periods evaluate selected slots in India time; unavailable hours or route durations remain unknown rather than being generated by the model.
- Keep the existing device storage key through rebrands so free-curation history is not reset, and disclose that QR payments are self-confirmed rather than verified.
